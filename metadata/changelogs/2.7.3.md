# Horse Powered 2.7.3

Creative Battery fix, plus common tags for flour and dough.

## Changes

- Flour is now tagged `c:flours` and `c:flours/wheat`, and Dough is tagged `c:doughs` and `c:doughs/wheat`, so other mods' recipes and Almost Unified treat them like Create's flour (breakinblocks/HorsePowered#26)

## Bug Fixes

- The Creative Battery now supplies unlimited power to adjacent blocks and to anything that pulls energy from it. Before, it started empty and never output anything
