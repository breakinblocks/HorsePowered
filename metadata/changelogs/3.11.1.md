# Horse Powered 3.11.1

Hotfix for the bottling feature from 3.11.0, plus Seed Oil, interaction, and rendering fixes.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed a crash when right-clicking the Horse Press with any item in hand.
- Bottling recipes now reach the client, so the JEI Bottling category is no longer empty and bottling works on servers.
- Spam right-clicking a station with items no longer makes them disappear from your hand (breakinblocks/HorsePowered#22). 
- Items added to a partly filled input slot are now saved properly. 
- Stack size labels are back on the Grindstone, Hand Grindstone, Chopper, and Press (breakinblocks/HorsePowered#23). 
- Seed Oil now behaves like a proper fluid. Swimming, splashing, and fall-damage reset work, mobs and players move through it normally instead of getting stuck.
