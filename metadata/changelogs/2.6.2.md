# Horse Powered 2.6.2

## Bug Fixes

- Spam right-clicking a station with items no longer makes them disappear from your hand ([#22](https://github.com/breakinblocks/HorsePowered/issues/22)).
- Items added to a partly filled input slot are now saved properly.
- Stack size numbers no longer get cut in half by the station they sit on, and they follow your Text Background accessibility setting ([#23](https://github.com/breakinblocks/HorsePowered/issues/23)).
- Seed Oil no longer plays stone break sounds, and boats float on it.
