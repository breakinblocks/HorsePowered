# Horse Powered 2.6.3

## Bug Fixes

- Horse presses no longer end up empty and refusing all items after spam right-clicking. Presses already stuck this way fix themselves ([#25](https://github.com/breakinblocks/HorsePowered/issues/25)).
- Right-clicking a station while holding an item no longer yanks back the item you just put in, so grindstones and choppers keep what you feed them ([#25](https://github.com/breakinblocks/HorsePowered/issues/25)).
- Right-clicking a station while holding an item no longer hands your worker back. Releasing a worker now needs an empty hand ([#25](https://github.com/breakinblocks/HorsePowered/issues/25)).
- Leading an animal near a station that already has a worker no longer stops you putting items in.
- Taking an item back out of a station now clears its progress instead of carrying it over to the next one.
