# Horse Powered 3.4.0

## New Content

- **Horse Powered Generator** — a new horse-powered machine that converts labor directly into Forge Energy (FE).
  - Generates **80 FE/tick** while a worker is walking
  - Internal buffer: **100,000 FE**
  - Output: up to **80 FE/tick** extracted from any side (output-only, does not accept energy)
  - Toggled on/off with a redstone signal (e.g. a lever on top)
  - The redstone torch on top lights up red when the generator is actively producing power
  - Recipe: Horse Grindstone in the center, a Lightning Rod on top, Polished Deepslate filling the rest, with two Polished Deepslate Slabs in the upper corners
